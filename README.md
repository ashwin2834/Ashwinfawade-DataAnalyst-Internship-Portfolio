### Statistical Analysis
A hypothesis test was performed to analyze whether there was a statistically significant difference in spending between male and female customers.

**T-Statistic:** 0.6826  
**P-Value:** 0.4950

The results indicated that the observed difference in average spending between the two groups was **not statistically significant** at the conventional 5% significance level.
